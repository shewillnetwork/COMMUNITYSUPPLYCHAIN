How the first location gets chosen

This isn't random, and it isn't just me deciding. Here's the actual loop:

1

Self-identify

Makers, drivers, warehouses, and store-location votes all show up on one map.

2

Reach the threshold

We need real numbers before anything launches — at least 50 shelf-ready brands, and a real base of local support in a city.

3

The city with the most backers goes first

Whichever city hits the mark first is the pilot. Not picked by me — picked by who shows up.

4

The fundraiser opens

Once a city is chosen, that's when the real fundraiser launches — to secure the location, the drivers, and the first inventory.

Why regional, not national

Shipping costs and fuel prices keep climbing — every mile a product travels costs more. Regional hubs keep sourcing, hauling, and spending close to home, which means the dollars stay circulating in the communities that raised them, instead of funding a truck route across the country.
